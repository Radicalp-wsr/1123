💡 MRO & Preventative Maintenance Strategy

Recommended field service intervals for Lectrodryer BAC-50 hydrogen auxiliary systems.

Service Manual: CE2003.02
EVERY 6 MONTHS 🛠️

4-Way Valve & Actuator Inspection

Inspect interlocked non-lubricated plug valves and pneumatic actuators. Test solenoid valve switching timing and verify stroke position switches.

EVERY 12 MONTHS 📡

Dewpoint Sensor Calibration

Calibrate digital ceramic moisture sensors against certified reference standard. Verify 4-20mA loop outputs and DCS high dewpoint alarm thresholds.

EVERY 3 TO 5 YEARS 🧪

Desiccant Bed Replacement

Drain activated alumina / molecular sieve charge from lower vessel nozzles. Refill with fresh high-capacity desiccant and replace internal stainless retention screens.
